## Concepts surfaced

- [[semantic-tokens]]: name colours/spacing by role (primary, success) not value, so one token drives many places.
- [[optical-alignment]]: nudge elements off mathematical centre so they look centred; 2 px shifts for icons in circles.
- [[component-api-design]]: props (size, variant, state, icon) as the contract of a reusable component.
- [[perceived-performance]]: skeleton, partial, progressive reveal so a 2 s load feels fast.
- [[non-deterministic-systems]]: same input yields a probability distribution of valid outputs; design for the distribution.
- [[progressive-disclosure]]: show less by default, reveal on demand (essential, expand, advanced).
- [[oklch]]: perceptually uniform colour space (lightness, chroma, hue) for consistent ramps.
- [[generative-ui]]: AI interprets intent and assembles interface from a component catalogue.
- [[model-latency-as-design-constraint]]: waiting / processing / response stages each need their own UI; time is part of the experience.
- [[design-engineering-culture]]: one product, one system, one team; designers and engineers share components, tokens, patterns, API.
- [[jobs-to-be-done]]: people hire products to get a job done; design for the job, not the product.
- [[motion-choreography]]: many elements, one sequence; staggered enter/follow/elevate/conclude on a 0-400 ms timeline.
- [[trust-and-error-states]]: AI outputs branch into confirmed / uncertain / error states along a low-to-high trust scale.
- [[design-debt]]: small shortcuts accumulate into duplicated, inconsistent, hard-to-maintain UI.
- [[craft]]: obsession in the details (typography, 8 px grid, pixel alignment, measurement) as a team function.
